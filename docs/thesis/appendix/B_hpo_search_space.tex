\chapter{Hyperparameter Optimisation Search Space}
\label{app:hpo-search-space}

\section{Framework}
\label{app:hpo-framework}

Optuna was used with the Tree-structured Parzen Estimator (TPE) sampler and
the MedianPruner \cite{akiba2019}. Trials were pruned at epoch 4 if the
validation AUC was below the median of completed trials.

\section{Search space}
\label{app:hpo-space}

    Label smoothing    & uniform on $[0,\, 0.1]$ \\
    Freeze fraction    & categorical in $\{0,\, 0.5\}$ \\
    Batch size         & categorical in $\{16,\, 32,\, 64\}$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
